# Setswana Corpus Transcription Guidelines

## General principle

Transcribe the speech as it was spoken, not as it should
be written in formal Setswana.

## Unclear speech

[uncertain: word]

## Completely inaudible speech

[inaudible]

## Non-speech events

[giggles]
[laughs]
[coughs]
[background noise]

... = pause or break in speech

## Code-switching

Keep words from English, Afrikaans, or other languages
when they occur naturally in the recording.

## Repetition

Keep repeated words.

## Informal speech

Do not correct informal grammar or pronunciation.
